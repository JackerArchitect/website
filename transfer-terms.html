2. On-Chain, Transparent, Public

Every transfer settles on Solana Mainnet. Every transaction is public, transparent, and independently verifiable by anyone, at any time, forever — via Solscan ↗. The chain is the ledger. Nothing is hidden, and nothing can be quietly altered.

3. The Patron Ledger

  • One seat = 100 USDC, sent as a one-time honor contribution.
  • After transferring, contact the Architect with your wallet address and the Document you back.
  • Your seat is recorded in the public Patron Ledger — wallet, seat number, and Document — published and permanently maintained.
  • When a Document's 49 seats are filled, that edition closes forever. Total capacity: 294 seats.

4. Order of Arrival, Allocation & Refunds

  • First come, first recorded. Seats are assigned strictly in the order transfers are confirmed on Solana Mainnet. Seat №1 is the first confirmed 100 USDC transfer. The ledger follows the chain — not the Architect.
  • 100 USDC = 1 seat. A transfer of N × 100 USDC claims N seats, while seats remain.
  • Closure. When a Document's 49 seats are filled, that edition closes permanently and intake stops for it.
  • Full refund of excess. Any remainder above whole seats, any transfer below 100 USDC, and any transfer received after closure is refunded in full — original amount, same asset (USDC), to the sender's address. The Architect bears all network fees.
  • Verification. Every allocation and every refund is an on-chain transaction, visible in the same public ledger.

5. Use of Funds

Contributions fund the research and development of the foundation layer: architecture, code audits, and long-term groundwork. Periodic transparency notes will be published. This wallet is personal — not a multisig, not a custodian, not a corporate entity — and this is stated plainly, by design.

6. Finality

On-chain transfers are irreversible. Refunds occur only as described in Section 4, at the Architect's initiative. No chargebacks exist on-chain, and none are promised beyond the stated refund rules.

7. Impostors & Phishing
